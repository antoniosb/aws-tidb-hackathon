# This folder is not used

Submissions do not go here. There is nothing to copy in, fork, or open a pull
request against.

**To submit:** comment on the pinned **Entregas** issue with your team name,
your public repository URL, and your demo link. That is the whole submission.

At the deadline, organizers fork every submitted repository. That fork is the
snapshot that gets judged — anything pushed after it is not scored.

See the [README](../README.md) and the
[participant guide](../PARTICIPANT-GUIDE.md) for the full brief.

*These team directories are left over from an earlier submission flow and are
kept only so old links do not break.*
